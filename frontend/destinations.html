How MaxAre works

From invitation to unforgettable memories.

The complete MaxAre experience can be used together — or each part can work independently.

01 Digital Invitation

Start with the invitation.

Create a personalized digital invitation for your event. Add all the information your guests need and let MaxAre handle the experience.

01

Create your event

Choose your event type and personalize the invitation.

02

Add event details

Date, location, dress code, schedule, gifts and other important information.

03

Share your invitation

Guests receive a unique invitation and can access it through the QR code.

04

Manage RSVP

See who can attend and keep track of your guest list.

02 Event Memories

The celebration ends. The memories stay.

Guests can upload the photos and videos they take during the event directly to the shared event gallery.

📱

Guests open the event's upload page directly from their phone.

📸

Upload photos & videos

Guests can easily share the moments they captured.

📺

Show them on a TV

The shared gallery can become a live slideshow during the event.

💾

Keep the memories

Event storage can remain available for a defined period, with optional extended storage later.
